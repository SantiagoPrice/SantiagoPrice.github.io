I am a research engineer passionate about developing novel mechatronic technologies that improve people's lives. I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the <a href='https://www.ai.iit.tsukuba.ac.jp/index.html'>Artificial Intelligence Laboratory</a>, University of Tsukuba, Japan, where I also earned my Ph.D. in Engineering. I received my master`s and bachelor degree in Engineering at <a href='https://www.ib.edu.ar/home-english-version/'> Instituto Balseiro </a>, Argentina. My research interests includes assistive robotics, human sensing, human-robot interaction, material science, and actuation technologies.
